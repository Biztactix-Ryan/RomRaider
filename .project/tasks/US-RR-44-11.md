- LEG-ENC-02: `MOVI20` decode of the `01 80 FE 0C` example (-459 252) and encode keeping the low nibble and opcode byte (UL-ENC-06) (§1.4). Exists in part: fixtures/kat/u20-movi20.toml, crates/rr-core/src/encoding/codec/tests.rs `u20_keeps_the_movi20_opcode_nibble`; add the worked example.
- LEG-ENC-06: masked write, `uint16` mask `00F0`, big (`A7 35`->`A7 55`) and little (`35 A7`->`55 A7`, legacy `75 07`, UL-ENC-03); value wider than the field refused (UL-ENC-04); signed field sign-extends (§3.1, §3.2).
- LEG-ENC-08: bitwise switch byte `2A`, bit 3 reads 1; set and undo change only bit 3 (legacy undo `72`, UL-ENC-05) (§3.3).
- LEG-ENC-10: rounding `x/0.5` at 10.25 -> 21, -10.25 -> -20; 20 000 on `int8` refused (§6.2). Exists in part: crates/rr-core/src/conversion/inverse/tests.rs.
- LEG-ENC-11: no `to_byte`, `x*0.5` on `uint8`, 10.6 -> 21 every call (MF-05); decreasing and non-monotonic; `uint32` and float storage (UL-ENC-07) (§6.3). Exists in part: crates/rr-core/tests/mf05_inverse.rs.
- LEG-ENC-12: `uint32` raw 3 000 000 000 and `C0000000` round trip (UL-ENC-01); `to_byte="1/(x-3)"` at 3 refused (UL-ENC-02) (§1.3, §6.2, §6.4).
- LEG-ENC-13: rr-defs `to_raw_mismatch`: a `to_byte` agreeing with `expression` only at raw 5, and one NaN there: `CNV0005` (UL-ENC-08) (§6.5).
- LEG-ENC-15: add to fixtures/kat/expr-jep-precedence.toml / expr-bitwise-functions.toml: `BitWise(24, 60, 1)` = 24, `BitWise(4, 240, 5)` = 15, `BitWise(1, 2147483648, 1)` = 1; `X`, `2x`, `0x18` refused at parse (§7.3, §7.4).
- LEG-ENC-16: a ROM scaling naming `pi`, `X` or a parameter is a load error (`CONVERSION_PARAM_REF`), not NaN per cell (UL-ENC-09, UL-ENC-10); NaN/inf `to_real` is a `ToRealError` (§5, §7.3).
